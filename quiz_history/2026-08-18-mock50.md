## Missed questions
1. **Atmosphere** — equatorial tropopause height/temperature (higher AND colder than polar, not "higher = warmer").
2. **Pressure** — layer thickness is greater in warm air, not cold.
3. **Wind** — surface friction backs and *slows* wind vs the gradient wind aloft (NH).
4. **Clouds** — Nimbostratus (not Stratocumulus) is the low-base genus extending through multiple levels.
5. **Stability** — inversions suppress vertical mixing, they don't encourage convection.
6. **Fronts** — cold occlusion: air *behind* the front is colder (cold-front character dominates).
7. **Fronts** — India's dominant winter air mass is Tropical Continental (Tc), not Tropical Maritime (Tm).
8. **Pressure Systems** — TRS energy source is latent heat release over warm ocean water, not solar heating.
