# Aviation Meteorology — DGCA CPL/IR Syllabus

Per Civil Aviation Requirement (CAR) Section 7, Series 'B', Part IV and Section J/O of
Schedule II, Aircraft Rules 1937 — in conformity with ICAO Annex 1 knowledge requirements.

Source: `Source/Meteorology Syllabus @cockpitchats (1).pdf`.

## 1. The Atmosphere

### 1.1 Composition, Extent, and Vertical Division
- **Temperature:** vertical distribution; transfer of heat (solar/terrestrial radiation, conduction,
  convection, advection, turbulence); lapse rate, stability, instability; inversions and their types;
  surface temperature — diurnal variation, effect of clouds and wind
- **Atmospheric Pressure:** barometric pressure and isobars; pressure variation with height and
  contours (isohypses); reduction of pressure to MSL, QFF; surface/upper-air low and high
- **Atmospheric Density:** interrelationship of pressure, temperature, density
- **International Standard Atmosphere (ISA)**
- **Altimetry:** pressure altitude and true altitude; height, altitude, flight level; altimeter settings
  QNH, QFE, 1013.25 hPa; effect of accelerated airflow due to topography

### 1.2 Wind
- Definition and measurement
- Primary cause of wind: pressure gradient, Coriolis force, gradient wind; isobars-to-wind relationship
- General circulation around the globe
- Turbulence and gustiness — types, origin, location
- Variation of wind with height, friction layer
- Local winds: anabatic/katabatic, land/sea breezes, venturi effects
- Standing waves — origin

### 1.3 Thermodynamics
- Humidity: water vapour in the atmosphere; temperature/dew point, mixing ratio, relative humidity

### 1.4 Clouds and Fog
- Cloud formation and description: types, classification; influence of inversions on cloud development
- Fog, mist, haze: radiation, advection, steaming, frontal, orographic fog

### 1.5 Precipitation
- Development of precipitation; types and their relationship with cloud types

### 1.6 Airmasses and Fronts
- Airmasses: description, factors affecting properties, classification, modifications, areas of origin
- Fronts: boundaries between airmasses, geographic differentiation; warm/cold front, associated
  clouds and weather; warm sector, weather behind cold front; occlusions, stationary front; movement
  of fronts and pressure systems, life cycle

### 1.7 Pressure Systems
- Principal pressure areas
- Anticyclones: types, general properties, cold/warm anticyclones, ridges/wedges, subsidence
- Non-frontal depressions: thermal, orographic, secondary depressions; cold air pools, troughs
- Tropical revolving storms

### 1.8 Climatology
- Typical mid-latitude weather: westerly waves, high-pressure areas, uniform pressure patterns
- Local seasonal weather and wind (India): SE & NE Monsoon, pre-monsoon, Northwesters,
  Kalbaisakhi; Western Disturbance

### 1.9 Flight Hazards
- Icing: conditions for ice accretion, topographical effects
- Turbulence: effects on flight, avoidance
- Windshear: definition, weather conditions, effects on flight
- Thunderstorms: structure, squall lines, life history, storm cells, atmospheric electricity, static
  charges; conditions for development, forecast, location, type; avoidance, ground/airborne radar,
  storm scope; downbursts, lightning discharge and impact on aircraft
- Low/high-level inversions: influence on aircraft performance
- Mountainous-area hazards: terrain effects on clouds/precipitation, frontal passage; vertical
  movements, mountain waves, windshear, turbulence, ice accretion; valley inversions
- Visibility-reducing phenomena: mist, smoke, dust, sand, precipitation, low drifting/blowing snow

### 1.10 Meteorological Information
- Observation: surface wind, visibility, RVR, transmissometers; clouds (type, amount, base/top
  height, movement); weather (precipitation, temperature, RH, dew point, pressure); aircraft
  observations/reporting, data link, PIREPs
- Weather charts: significant weather, surface, upper-air; symbols on analyzed/prognostic charts
- Flight-planning information: aeronautical codes (METAR, TAF, SPECI, SIGMET, AIRMET, SNOWTAM,
  runway report); broadcasts (VOLMET, ATIS, HF VOLMET, ACARS); pre-flight documents, briefing,
  advice; low-level windshear/inversion warning systems; special meteorological warnings
