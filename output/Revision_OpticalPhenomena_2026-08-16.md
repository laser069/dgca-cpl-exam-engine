## DGCA Important Facts
- Halo ↔ **High cloud (CS)**, ice crystals, negligible icing chance.
- Corona ↔ **Altostratus**, water droplets, moderate icing risk if above freezing level.
- Bishop's Ring looks like a halo (~22°) but is caused by **dust**, not ice — distinguish by cause.
- Primary rainbow = **42°**, red outside/violet inside; secondary = **52°**, colours reversed, fainter, outside the primary.
- Glory = pilot's own-shadow phenomenon on cloud/fog tops — commonly seen from the aircraft, not a ground observation.
- Inferior mirage = hot surface, steep lapse rate; superior mirage = cold surface, temperature inversion.

## 60-Second Revision Pass
1. Ring **under 5°**, dull colours → **Corona → Altostratus → moderate icing**.
2. Ring **22° or 46°**, bright → **Halo → Cirrostratus → negligible icing**.
3. Ring **~22°** but caused by **dust** → **Bishop's Ring**.
4. Arc **42°**, red-out/violet-in → **Primary rainbow**. Fainter arc **52°**, reversed colours → **Secondary rainbow**.
5. Rings around **your own shadow** on cloud/fog → **Glory**.
6. Shimmer **below** object over **hot ground** → **Inferior mirage**. Shimmer **above** object over **cold surface/inversion** → **Superior mirage**.
