## Definition

- The Aircraft Act 1934: an Act for the control of manufacture, possession, use, operation, sale, import and export of aircraft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- The Aircraft Rules 1937: rules made under the Act to regulate civil aviation in India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- The Indian Aircraft Rules 1920: customs rules for aircraft arriving in or departing from India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- The Aircraft (Public Health) Rules 1954: rules made to protect public health (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- DGCA is the regulator that administers these rules; Central Government holds licensing powers and delegates some to the DGCA (src: air_regulations_fair; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Formation

Background / how it works:

- Act applicability: extends to the whole of India; applies to Indian citizens wherever they are; to aircraft registered in India wherever they are; to aircraft registered outside India but in or over India; and to an aircraft operated by a non-citizen with principal place of business or permanent residence in India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Central Government may by notification exempt any aircraft, class of aircraft, person or class of persons (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Detention: an authority authorised by the Central Government may detain an aircraft if (a) the nature of the intended flight would involve danger to persons or property, or (b) detention is needed to secure compliance with the Act (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Rules: no aircraft may fly until registered and displaying its registration mark (src: air_regulations_fair; air_regulations_quick_notes).
- Flight plan and communications: flight plan must be filed 60 minutes before departure for controlled flights; in flight 10 minutes before reaching the entry point; delay of 30 min (controlled) or 1 hour (uncontrolled) requires an amended or new plan (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).

## DGCA Important Facts

**Penalties (Aircraft Act, as stated in notes)**
- False information on carriage of arms, explosives or dangerous goods: up to 2 years imprisonment and fine up to Rs 10 lakh; if the offender is not the owner, the owner is also punishable (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Slaughter/flaying of animals or depositing rubbish within 10 km of the aerodrome reference point: up to 3 years or fine up to Rs 10 lakh, or both (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Flying so as to cause danger: air_regulations_fair gives imprisonment up to 2 years (src: air_regulations_fair) — CONFLICT with the question-bank option sets in the pool and air_regulations_2 that say 6 months / Rs 1000. Do not state the flying-danger penalty without checking a primary source.
- Abetting the offence: same punishment as the principal (src: air_regulations_fair).
- Dangerous flying (rule 21): no person may fly so as to cause unnecessary danger to persons or property (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Demolition: if a person fails to demolish or reduce a building, structure or tree as directed under section 9A notification, an officer may do it (section 11B) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

**Flight and crew conduct**
- Alcohol and psychoactive substances: no crew member (pilot, navigator, engineer, cabin crew) may take alcoholic drink or sedative, narcotic or stimulant within 12 hours before the commencement of the flight (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 24; air_regulations_fair "12 hours"). Passengers may drink but may not board intoxicated (src: air_regulations_fair).
- Assault, intimidation or threats against a crew member on board are prohibited (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 22).
- Mental disorder or epilepsy: a person may be carried only if a registered medical practitioner certifies fitness to travel by air (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 24A).
- Minimum age for sole control of an aircraft in motion: 16 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 28).
- Maximum age: a licence holder aged 65 or above may not act as PIC or co-pilot in commercial air transport (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 28A).
- Sonic boom: no civil aircraft may operate at true Mach above 1 over India or the high seas in a way likely to cause sonic boom over India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 29A).
- Portable electronic devices: not in flight, except cellular phones after landing and clearing the active runway, at the PIC's permission (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 29B).
- Safety Management Systems: establish and maintain; prepare SMS Manual for DG approval (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 29D).
- Flight navigator: a public transport aircraft flying more than 600 NM great-circle without landing, and without navigation equipment giving instant and continuous position, must carry one (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

**Carriage rules**
- Arms, ammunition and explosives: carriage prohibited except with written permission of the Central Government (src: air_regulations_fair; zenithaviation_regsnotes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav "Central Government" for exceptions).
- Military personnel carrying arms on civil aircraft: written permission of the Central Government (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes; air_regulations_fair).
- Prisoners: may be taken abroad or carried only under a written permit from the Director-General, a Deputy Director-General, the Director of Regulations and Information, or another authorised civil aviation officer (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 24B). Zenith adds CAD (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Animals, birds and reptiles: DG written permit required (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 24C).
- Mail: carried only with DG Posts and Telegraph permission (src: air_regulations_fair; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Photographs of aerodromes from an aircraft in flight: DGCA permission (src: air_regulations_fair; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Dropping articles: nothing except ballast of fine sand or water may be dropped from an aircraft in motion; printed matter or petals only with written permission of the District Magistrate or Commissioner of Police (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 26; air_regulations_fair). Parachuting only with Central Government permission (src: air_regulations_fair). Fuel may be dumped in emergency (src: air_regulations_fair).
- No person may be carried on the wings or undercarriage (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 27).

**Fuelling, housing and smoking**
- Fuelling outdoors, not less than 15 m from any building (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 25A).
- Smoking or naked flame, or spark-producing appliances, not within 30 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- No person other than operator staff, fuelling company, CAD, Customs and Police within 15 m of the aircraft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Turbo-jet rear outlets within 43 m of fuelling equipment: fuelling stops (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Engines shut down, ignition off during fuelling (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Aircraft with dangerous petroleum in bulk: hangar must be non-inflammable and ventilated (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 25B).

**Restricted and prohibited areas**
- Towers of Silence, Malabar Hills: 1 mile radius (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_fair).
- Baroda aerodrome: ground to unlimited upper level (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Rashtrapati Bhavan: ground to unlimited upper level (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Mathura Refinery: 10 km radius, ground to unlimited upper level (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_quick_notes lists it as prohibited).
- Bhubaneshwar: ground to 50,000 ft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Kalpakkam nuclear: 10 km radius, ground to 10,000 ft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Prohibited area = airspace over land/territorial waters where flight is totally prohibited. Restricted area = flight restricted to specified conditions (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).

**Public health (Aircraft (Public Health) Rules 1954)**
- Commander must inform the health officer at the destination at least 2 hours in advance by R/T of any person suffering from a quarantinable or infectious disease (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes; air_regulations_fair).
- Incubation periods (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav): influenza/pneumonia 5 days; cholera 5; yellow fever 6; plague 6; diphtheria 7; relapsing fever 8; cerebrospinal meningitis 10; small pox, chicken pox, typhus 14. Zenith gives cholera 5, plague 6, yellow fever 6, relapsing fever 8, small pox 14, typhus 14 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Dead bodies or remains that cannot be brought into India: died of yellow fever, plague, anthrax or glanders (zenith: "PAYG") (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes). Cremated ashes may be brought (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Uncremated remains: hermetically sealed in zinc container (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes); other remains brought in a suitable coffin (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Cholera is not in the "cannot be brought" list in notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav (see Common Mistakes).

**Licence and operation (DGCA side; full detail in Licensing)**
- Licensing authority: Central Government; DGCA exercises the functions (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_fair).
- Operations Manual: prepared by the operator and approved by the DGCA (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Route guides, Operations Manual, MEL: carried on aircraft in scheduled service (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 140B-140C).
- Accident notice: sent as soon as possible and in any case within 24 hours (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 68).
- Wreckage removal: only to extricate persons, prevent fire or obstruction, or other stated purposes (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 70).
- Log books: entries in ink; certified quarterly at end of March, June, September and December; retained 5 years after last entry (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 67A).
- Flight duty limits: see Licensing note. Maximum 1000 hours in 365 days; 125 hours in 30 days (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Cabin crew: 1 for 10-49 seats; 2 for 50-99; 3 for 100-149; 4 for 150-199; 5 for 200-249; 6 for 250-299 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Seat and flight deck admission: only crew members, certifying authority representatives, or persons permitted by the Operations Manual; final decision with the commander (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Air Defence Clearance (ADC): not needed within 5 NM of the aerodrome reference point and below 1000 ft; valid for the whole route; new ADC if departure delayed more than 30 minutes; request 10 minutes before entering an ADIZ (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Military/state aircraft: aircraft used for military, customs and police services are state aircraft; the Chicago Convention does not apply to them (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- Central Government vs DGCA vs District Magistrate vs Commissioner of Police vs DG Posts: who grants which permission.

- Public health incubation periods and dead-body restrictions (yellow fever, plague, anthrax, glanders).

- Fuelling distances: building 15 m, persons 15 m, smoking/naked flame 30 m, jet blast 43 m.
- Alcohol: 12 hours before flight.
- Flight navigator at 600 NM great-circle without landing.

## Common Mistakes

- **Demolition rules:** Rules 1994 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav), not 1937 (Aircraft Rules) or 1954 (Public Health). Pool letter A is model-derived and conflicts with this.
- **Public Health Rules 1954 vs Aircraft Rules 1937:** the health rules are the 1954 instrument (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Pool Q "Rules governing public health" shows derived C (Aircraft Rules 1954), which agrees (src: air_regulations_2 Q82 question; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Prisoner permission:** DGCA/DDG/DRI (civil aviation officers) — not District Magistrate, not State Government, not Central Government (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav rule 24B; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Arms permission:** Central Government for military and carriage of arms/explosives, not DGCA (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes; air_regulations_fair). Distractor "DGCA" is wrong for arms.
- **Leaflets/printed matter:** District Magistrate or Commissioner of Police written permission (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav rule 26). DGCA/DDGCA/DR&I distractors are wrong.
- **Yellow fever vs cholera:** yellow fever (and plague, anthrax, glanders) remains cannot be brought into India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Cholera does not appear in that list. air_regulations_2 Q23 and Q117 are two cholera questions with different option sets and no key in the source, so do not assume a cholera answer.
- **Incubation periods:** yellow fever 6 days vs relapsing fever 8 days vs typhus/small pox/chicken pox 14 days (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Health officer warning time:** 2 hours in advance by R/T (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes). Do not use 48 or 24 hours from distractors.
- **Alcohol:** 12 hours before commencement of the flight (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav rule 24). Pool distractor "24 hours" is not the rule in that source.
- **Penal figures:** flying-danger penalty conflicts across sources (air_regulations_fair: 2 years; pool options: 6 months / Rs 1000). Arms false-information penalty is 2 years + Rs 10 lakh (notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Do not mix them.
- **Fuelling distances:** building/persons 15 m; smoking 30 m; jet blast 43 m. Do not swap 30 m (smoking) with 15 m (building) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Prohibited vs restricted:** Mathura Refinery 10 km is listed as a prohibited area in quick notes; restricted areas are a separate category (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Duty time vs flight time:** 1000 hours is the annual flight-time limit (rule-level), not the duty-time limit (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Log book retention:** 5 years (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav rule 67A).
