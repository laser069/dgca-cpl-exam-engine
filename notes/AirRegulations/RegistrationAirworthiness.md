## Definition

- Nationality mark: letters identifying the country of registration. Registration mark: letters/numbers following it, identifying the particular aircraft (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, Annex 7 definitions).
- Airworthiness: the ability of an aircraft to be operated in flight and on the ground without significant hazard to aircrew, ground crew, passengers or third parties (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, Annex 8).
- Certificate of Registration (CofR): proves the aircraft is registered in a State. Given by the Central Government in India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
- Certificate of Airworthiness (CofA): certifies that the aircraft meets airworthiness requirements and may be flown. Issued/validated by the Director-General for Indian aircraft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, National Provisions; air_regulations_quick_notes).
- Type Certificate: issued by the State of Design to define the type design and signify approval (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Formation

Background / how it works:

- Annex 7 sets how States choose nationality marks from the radio call-sign symbols allocated by the ITU. India was allotted AT to AW by ITU, but continues to use VT (civil) and VU (defence) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Annex 7 registration mark: letters, numbers or combination, assigned by the State of Registry or common mark registering authority (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_4 Q74 matched).
- Annex 8 national provisions: owner or operator applies to the Director-General for a CofA, or for validation of a CofA issued elsewhere. DG may validate a CofA for an imported aircraft if the manufacturing State's authority has issued one (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- A CofA remains in force subject to conditions the DG specifies; DG may require inspection or flight test (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- DG may cancel, suspend or endorse a Type Certificate where a defect threatens safety (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Register of Indian aircraft kept by the Director-General (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## DGCA Important Facts

**Marks**
- Wings (heavier-than-air): marks at least 50 cm (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh; air_regulations_4 Q79 matched).
- Fuselage (or equivalent) and vertical tail surfaces (heavier-than-air): at least 30 cm (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh; air_regulations_4 Q80 matched).
- Lighter-than-air (other than unmanned free balloons): at least 50 cm; unmanned free balloons per State of Registry (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Nationality mark: capital letters VT (civil), VU (defence); hyphen between nationality and registration marks (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
- Registration mark: letters, numbers or combination, 1 to 5 digits or characters (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Nirmal Firosh notes say "a group of three letters ... assigned by DGCA" (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh) — conflict, see Common Mistakes.
- Forbidden in registration marks: Q-codes, SOS, XXX, PAN, TTT (letter combinations confusable with International Code of Signals five-letter groups, Q-code three letters, and distress/urgent signals) (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_4 Q75-Q77 matched).
- VT-ALA is an example of a five-letter Indian registration mark (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Marks must be clean and visible; operator logo must not interfere with the mark (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).

**Registration**
- Dual registration not allowed; registration may be changed from one State to another (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Leased aircraft: CofR must include lease validity and names, nationalities and addresses of lessor and lessee (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Nationality and registration marks cancelled by DGCA only when the aircraft is destroyed or sold to another country (src: air_regulations_quick_notes). Zenith: CofR valid until the aircraft is destroyed (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- CofR must be carried on board at all times (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; pool derived D matches).
- Documents to carry (Vinod list, condensed): CofR; CofA; ARC; noise certificate; Air Operator's Permit; crew licences; aero-mobile radio licence; journey log; Operations Manual; MEL; Flight Manual; cabin crew manual; cockpit and emergency checklists; CRS; LOPA; emergency equipment layout; route guides; charts; weight schedule; load and trim sheet; passenger and cargo lists; dangerous goods list (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Zenith's shorter list adds Medical Licence and Logbook (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).

**Certificate of Airworthiness**
- CofA validity by aircraft age (src: air_regulations_quick_notes): age 1 to 10 years = valid 5 years; age 10 to 20 years = valid 2 years; after age 20 = valid 1 year.
- CofA remains in force for the period specified in it and may be renewed by the DG (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 50).
- Continuing validity depends on the laws of the State of Registry (src: air_regulations_4 Q59, Q69, Q71 matched). Continuing airworthiness is determined by the State of Registry (src: air_regulations_4 Q60, Q68 matched).
- CofA is issued by the Contracting State that approves the aircraft (src: air_regulations_4 Q62 matched). Indian rules: DG issues/validates (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Standards in Annex 8 apply to aircraft of more than 5700 kg MTOM; the Annex 8 standard for engines is 2 or more (src: air_regulations_4 Q63, Q64 matched).
- Aircraft damaged in a foreign State may fly without fare-paying passengers to a repair airport only with permission of the State of Registry (src: air_regulations_4 Q56, Q61 matched).
- Smoking in aircraft is permitted or prohibited as written in the CofA (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Flight data recorder (FDR): records about 25 hours of data; starts when aircraft moves under its own power; orange; mounted in the tail (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Cockpit voice recorder (CVR): 30 min to 2 hours (zenith).

**Operating and maintenance records**
- Flight Manual approved by the State of Design (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- MEL approved by DGCA; enables PIC to decide whether flight may start or continue with equipment inoperative (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Crew log books preserved for 5 years from date of last entry (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- Marks: heights 50 cm (wings, lighter-than-air) vs 30 cm (fuselage, tail).
- Mark format: VT (civil) vs VU (defence); hyphen; letters/numbers; forbidden codes (SOS, PAN, TTT, XXX).
- Who assigns the registration mark: State of Registry or common mark registering authority (Annex 7).
- CofR vs CofA: CofR = registration/ownership; CofA = airworthiness; CofA validity depends on age and on the laws of the State of Registry.
- Annex 7 = Standards only (air_regulations_4 Q86 matched); Annex 8 = airworthiness standards.
- Airworthiness responsibility: State of Registry for continuing airworthiness.
- Starboard/port light arcs (navigation lights) are often grouped with registration questions in the pool; see Memory Questions.

## Common Mistakes

- **Wings 50 cm vs fuselage 30 cm:** the larger height goes on the wings; the fuselage and tail get 30 cm (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_4 Q79, Q80 matched).
- **Registration mark length:** Vinod says 1 to 5 characters (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav); Nirmal Firosh says three letters (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh). Use the Annex 7 wording (1 to 5) for Annex questions.
- **Nationality mark:** VT = civil; VU = defence; AT to AW = ITU-allotted symbols that India does not currently use as its mark (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Forbidden marks:** SOS, PAN, TTT, XXX and Q-codes (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_4 Q75-Q77). "SY-PAN" contains PAN (air_regulations_4 Q82).
- **CofR vs CofA:** CofR is held for the aircraft's registration and must be carried on board (src: air_regulations_quick_notes); CofA is airworthiness and is time-limited by age (src: air_regulations_quick_notes). Do not treat CofA as a registration document, and vice versa.
- **Certificate issuer:** DG / DGCA for Indian aircraft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav); Central Government for registration (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav); Contracting State that approves the aircraft for the CofA as an ICAO statement (src: air_regulations_4 Q62 matched).
- **Annex 7 vs Annex 8:** Annex 7 = marks and registration; Annex 8 = airworthiness (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **CofA validity:** the age bands are 1-10 (5 years), 10-20 (2 years), above 20 (1 year). Do not confuse with licence validity (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Cancellation of registration:** only when destroyed or sold to another country (quick notes). Zenith says valid until destroyed. Pool distractors that say "change of ownership" lapse the CofA are not supported by the sources read.
- **Navigation light arcs:** starboard green 110 degrees; port red 110 degrees; rear white 140 degrees; 70 degrees is the coverage either side of the axis (src: air_regulations_quick_notes). Do not mix 70/110/140.
- **Annex 7 "Standards only" vs pool SARPS:** the matched key says Standards only; the pool's derived letter says SARPS. Use the key.
