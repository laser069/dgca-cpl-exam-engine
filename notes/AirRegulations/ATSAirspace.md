## Definition

- **Air Traffic Services (ATS) airspace**: "Airspace of defined dimensions alphabetically designated, within which specific types of flights may operate and for which air traffic services and rules of operation are specified." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Controlled airspace**: "Airspace of defined dimensions within which air traffic control service is provided to IFR flights and to VFR flights in accordance with the airspace classification." It covers ATS airspace classes A, B, C, D and E. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Control Zone (CTR)**: "A controlled airspace extending upwards from the surface of the earth to a specified upper limit." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Control Area (CTA/TMA)**: "A controlled airspace extending upwards from a specified limit above the earth." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Controlled aerodrome**: "An aerodrome at which air traffic control service is provided to aerodrome traffic." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Air Traffic Advisory Service**: "A service provided within advisory airspace to ensure separation, as far as practical, between aircraft which are operating on IFR flight plans. (Provided only in F type of airspaces)" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Alerting service**: "A service provided to notify appropriate organizations regarding aircraft in need of search and rescue aid, and to assist such organizations as required." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Prohibited area**: "An airspace of defined dimensions, above the land areas or territorial waters of a State, within which the flight of aircraft is totally prohibited." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Restricted area**: "An airspace of defined dimensions ... within which the flight of aircraft is restricted in accordance with certain specified conditions." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Danger area**: "An airspace of defined dimensions within which activities dangerous to the flight of aircraft may exist at specified times." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Classification

ATS airspace classes (source wording):

- **Class A**: Only IFR flights permitted; all flights subject to ATC and separated from each other. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Class B**: IFR and VFR permitted; all flights subject to ATC and separated from each other. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Class C**: IFR and VFR permitted; all subject to ATC. IFR separated from IFR and from VFR; VFR separated from IFR and receives information about other VFR. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Class D**: IFR and VFR permitted; all subject to ATC. IFR separated from other IFR and receive traffic information on VFR; VFR receive traffic information on all other flights. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Class E**: IFR and VFR permitted; IFR subject to ATC and separated from other IFR; all flights receive traffic information as far as practicable. Class E is not used for CTRs. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Class F**: IFR and VFR permitted; participating IFR flights receive an ATC advisory service; all flights receive flight information service if requested. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Class G**: IFR and VFR permitted; receive flight information service if requested. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Airspace classes are classified in AIP India ENR 1.4. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)

Controlled vs uncontrolled (as stated in the zenith note): "Airspace A to D is generally considered Controlled AD and E to G are considered Uncontrolled AD." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Formation

How ATS airspace is built up (background from the sources):

- Controlled airspace is designated as a layered set: aerodrome control tower (ATZ) for take-off/landing; approach control (CTR) for arrival/departure; area control (CTA/TMA) for en-route/airway flying; flight information service and alerting service across the rest. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- The CTR is built to contain the IFR arrival and departure paths; its lateral limit extends at least 5 NM (9.3 km) from the aerodrome centre. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- A CTA's lower limit is not less than 200 m (700 ft) above MSL or ground (whichever higher; some aerodromes can be below sea level). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- A TMA is "normally established at the confluence of ATS routes in the vicinity of one or more major aerodromes." (src: air_regulations_4)
- An airway is "a control area or portion thereof established in the form of a corridor equipped with navigation aids." (src: air_regulations_4)
- ATS routes outside CTAs/TMAs/CTRs/ATZs are Class E (except ATS routes segmented as Class F); airspace outside CTAs/TMAs/CTRs/ATZs is Class F. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## DGCA Important Facts

- Controlled-airspace class list in the source set: Class A, B, C, D, E are controlled airspace (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Uncontrolled airspace: E, F, G (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Separation by ATC between IFR and VFR flights is provided in Classes B and C (src: air_regulations_4).
- Continuous two-way radio for VFR flights is required in Classes B, C and D (src: air_regulations_4).
- Speed limit below 10,000 ft (India): IAS not greater than 250 kt; arriving aircraft within 15 NM of VOR/DME serving the aerodrome: IAS not greater than 220 kt. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Speed limit in the zenith note: "Max 250 knots IAS below FL 100 (From airspace C (VFR) till G)". (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- ATC clearance: obtain it at least 10 minutes before entering controlled airspace. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Entering another FIR: inform the ATS unit at least 10 minutes before entry to receive FIS. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- ADIZ: all aircraft need ADC 10 minutes before entering the ADIZ. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Flight plan (controlled flight) filed at least 10 minutes before departure. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Alerting phases: uncertainty (INCERFA) after 30 minutes with no communication or no arrival within 30 minutes of ETA; alert (ALERFA) after failed attempts, or cleared to land but not landed within 5 minutes of ETL; distress (DETRESFA) when fuel is exhausted or forced landing is likely. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Alerting phases: "Uncertainty phase, alert phase, distress phase." (src: air_regulations_4)
- Alerting service provided by the ATS unit responsible for the aircraft at that moment. (src: air_regulations_4)
- Within a flight information region, the services required are flight information service and alerting service. (src: air_regulations_4)
- Trigger of the alert phase: responsibility of air traffic control and flight information centres. (src: air_regulations_4)
- Minimum VMC for VFR in controlled airspace: see RulesOfTheAir (zenith table: A-E at and above FL100 = 8 km visibility, 1000 ft vertical, 1500 m horizontal; F and G below FL100 = 5 km). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Prohibited areas in India include: Bombay (Tower of Silence, 1 mile radius, unlimited height; VA(P)-2); Baroda (nuclear reactor); Mathura refinery (10 km radius); Rashtrapati Bhawan (VVIP movements). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Restricted areas in India use codes like (R)-81 (Restricted Area 81, Chennai FIR); prohibited areas use VA(P)-2 (Prohibited Area 2, Mumbai FIR). (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- NOTAM: "a notice distributed by means of telecommunications containing information concerning the establishment, condition or change in any aeronautical facility, service, procedure or hazard" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- AIP Supplement: temporary changes to the AIP published by special pages; long-term is more than 3 months, short-term less than 3 months (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- AIRAC: system for advance notification, on common effective dates, of changes that necessitate significant changes in operating practices (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Flight information service includes provision of pertinent information (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Special VFR: a VFR flight cleared by ATC to operate within a CTR (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Special VFR in a CTR may be authorized when ground visibility is not less than 1500 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- **Which class has what separation**: A and B: all flights subject to ATC and separated from each other. C: IFR separated from IFR and from VFR; VFR get information on other VFR. D: IFR separated from IFR; VFR get traffic information. E: IFR separated from IFR. F: advisory service for IFR. G: flight information only. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Class names**: "Class C ... IFR flights are separated from other IFR flights and from VFR flights." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Service types**: ATC service (separation), advisory service (F), FIS (information), alerting service (SAR notify). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Control zone vs control area**: CTR extends from surface to specified upper limit; CTA is from specified lower limit upward. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Prohibited vs restricted vs danger**: total prohibition vs conditional restriction vs hazard at specified times. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Speed control**: 250 kt IAS below 10,000 ft (India) / 250 kt below FL100 (zenith); 220 kt within 15 NM of VOR/DME for arrivals. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Notification**: NOTAM for temporary changes; AIRAC for scheduled changes at fixed effective dates. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_4)
- **FIR transfer**: notify ATS unit before entry to get FIS (10 min, zenith). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Common Mistakes

- **Control zone vs control area**: CTR = from the surface upward to a specified upper limit (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). CTA = from a specified lower limit (not less than 200 m / 700 ft) upward (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Do not swap "from surface" and "from a specified height".
- **Class C vs Class D**: in Class C, IFR is separated from VFR; in Class D, VFR gets only traffic information about IFR (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Class E in CTRs**: Class E is not used for CTRs (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Advisory service**: ATC advisory service is Class F only; Class G gets flight information service only (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Speed limit reference**: the sources state the 250 kt IAS limit with different references: "below 10,000 ft" (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav) and "below FL 100" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Match the reference the question uses and do not mix the two.
- **Rashtrapati Bhavan prohibited area**: zenithaviation_regsnotes lists it as "50000'" height, while notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav says "from ground level to an unlimited upper level". Conflict in the sources; check the AIP.
- **Prohibited vs restricted**: prohibited = totally prohibited; restricted = flight allowed under conditions or with permission (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Alerting phases**: INCERFA (uncertainty) is at 30 minutes without communication; ALERFA (alert) is the next stage; DETRESFA (distress) is when the fuel is exhausted or a forced landing is likely (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Do not use "alert" for the uncertainty phase.
- **Mode C tolerance**: +/- 300 ft (src: air_regulations_4) - do not mix with the 1000 ft RVSM separation (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **FIR entry**: 10 minutes before entry (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Pool options contain other values; check the source before using them.

## Revision Summary

- Controlled airspace: A to E (CTR, CTA/TMA, ATZ, airways). Uncontrolled: E to G per the zenith mapping. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Class A: IFR only, all separated. Class B: IFR and VFR, all separated. Class C: IFR separated from IFR and VFR. Class D: IFR separated from IFR; VFR gets TI. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Class E: not used for CTRs; TI as far as practicable. Class F: advisory service. Class G: FIS only. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- CTR: from surface to a specified upper limit, lateral at least 5 NM. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- CTA: lower limit not less than 200 m (700 ft). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Speed: 250 kt IAS below 10,000 ft; 220 kt within 15 NM of VOR/DME for arrivals. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- ATC clearance: at least 10 minutes before entering controlled airspace. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- FIR entry: notify ATS 10 minutes before entry for FIS. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Alerting phases: INCERFA (30 min no comms), ALERFA (landing not achieved within 5 min), DETRESFA (fuel exhausted/forced landing likely). (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Prohibited areas: totally prohibited; restricted: conditions/permission; danger: hazard at specified times. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
