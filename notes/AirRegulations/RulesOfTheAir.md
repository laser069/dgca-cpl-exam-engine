## Definition

- **Right of way**: "the right to proceed without alteration of course. The aircraft that has the right of way is required to maintain its heading and speed, and observe the other aircraft whilst the collision risk exists." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Overtaking aircraft**: "an aircraft that approaches another from the rear on a line forming an angle of less than 70 degrees with the plane of symmetry of the latter." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Flight by night**: "A flight performed between half an hour after sunset and half an hour before sunrise." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **VFR flight**: "shall be conducted so that the aircraft is flown in conditions of visibility and distance from clouds equal to or greater than those specified in Table." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Formation flight**: "Aircraft shall not be flown in formation except by prearrangement among the pilots-in-command of the aircraft taking part in the flight and, for formation flight in controlled airspace, in accordance with the conditions prescribed by the appropriate ATS authority." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Aerobatic flight**: "Manoeuvres intentionally performed by an aircraft involving an abrupt change in its attitude, an abnormal attitude, or an abnormal variation in speed." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Classification

- **Flight rules**: IFR vs VFR. IFR is "flown whenever weather is below VMC and when Pilot wishes to fly IFR but with proper navigational equipment's on board" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Cruising level systems**: Semi-circular system (magnetic tracks) below FL290; RVSM applied between FL290 and FL410 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
- **Right-of-way situations**: head-on, converging, overtaking, landing, emergency, surface movement (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Flight time of day**: day VFR window vs night (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Special cases**: formation, aerobatics, low flying, minimum heights (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).

## Formation

How the rules are applied in practice (background as stated in sources):

- Controlled flights must keep two-way RTF with the controlling ATC unit (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Rules are applied by the pilot-in-command, who has "final authority as to the disposition of the aircraft while in command" (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Right-of-way rules are a collision-avoidance set: the aircraft that "has the other on its right" gives way in converging; each aircraft in head-on turns right; the overtaken aircraft keeps its heading and the overtaker alters to the right (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Cruising levels are chosen by magnetic track, so the same track rule governs every flight on that track band (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- VMC minima are a table keyed to airspace class and altitude; VFR flights must stay at or beyond those values (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4).

## DGCA Important Facts

- Refuelling distances (zenith table): building 15 m; person 15 m; smoking/naked flame 30 m; jet blast 43 m. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Refuelling distances (fair notes): fuelling at least 15 m from building and unauthorized people; smoking and naked flame not permitted within 30 m. (src: air_regulations_fair)
- Person must remain at least 200 ft from a jet engine. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Highest available flight level for flying in India: FL 450; maximum flight level FL 460; lowest flight level FL 050. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Semi-circular rule, magnetic tracks: 0-179 magnetic track: IFR odd flight levels, VFR odd FL + 500 ft; 180-359: even flight levels (VFR even + 500 ft). (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Semi-circular rule applies below FL290; RVSM applies from FL290 to FL410. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_quick_notes)
- RVSM: separation reduced from 2000 ft to 1000 ft between FL290 and FL410. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh)
- Above FL290 (non-RVSM context in sources): minimum separation for same-track aircraft 4000 ft; RVSM same-track 2000 ft. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh)
- VFR must not operate above FL150. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- VFR must not operate more than 100 NM seaward from the shoreline within controlled airspace. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- VFR over congested areas of cities/towns or an open-air assembly: not lower than 300 m (1000 ft) above the highest obstacle within a 600 m radius. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- VFR elsewhere: not lower than 150 m (500 ft) above ground or water. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- VFR take-off/landing or entry into the aerodrome traffic zone in a CTR is not allowed when the ceiling is below 1500 ft or the ground visibility is below 5 km (unless cleared by ATC). (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4)
- VFR operating window: from 20 minutes before sunrise to 20 minutes after sunset (exceptions for ATC-cleared local flights). (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Minimum IFR levels: over high terrain or mountains, at least 600 m (2000 ft) above the highest obstacle within 8 km of the estimated position; elsewhere 300 m (1000 ft) above that obstacle. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- VMC minima above 3050 m (10000 ft) AMSL, all classes: visibility 8 km, 1500 m horizontal and 1000 ft vertical from cloud. (src: air_regulations_4)
- VMC minima below 3050 m for Class B are 5 km flight visibility with 1500 m horizontal distance from cloud (air_regulations_4 lists 300 m vertical in its Class B answers). (src: air_regulations_4)
- Aerobatics: not within 2 NM of the nearest point of the aerodrome perimeter unless above 1800 m / 6000 ft AGL; must be completed at not less than 2000 ft AGL; 600 m horizontal separation from built-up areas; no aerobatics over cities/towns/villages without written DG permission. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Overtaking angle: less than 70 degrees with the plane of symmetry. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Converging at approximately same level: aircraft with the other on its right shall give way. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Head-on: each aircraft alters heading to the right. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Overtaken aircraft has right of way; overtaking aircraft keeps out of the way by altering heading to the right. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Landing priority: aircraft with an emergency has the right of way; faster aircraft gives way to slower aircraft when both are coming in to land. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Power-driven aircraft give way to airships, balloons, gliders; gliders give way to balloons; power-driven aircraft give way to towing aircraft. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Navigation lights: red light port side (to 110 degrees left of dead ahead), green starboard (to 110 degrees right), white rear light through 140 degrees. Displayed sunset to sunrise. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Formation flight: only by pre-arrangement among PICs; zenith notes state "No formation Flights for Civil Aircraft"; fair notes state formation with permission from ATC, never with passengers. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_fair)
- Night (for aircraft lights/night definition): half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Airspace prohibited/restricted/danger areas: flight in a prohibited area is totally prohibited; restricted areas allow flight only under stated conditions or permission. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Aircraft shall not be flown in a prohibited or restricted area except per published conditions or with State permission. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- **Right of way hierarchy**: emergency first; then landing traffic; then the rule set (head-on, converging, overtaking). Converging: the aircraft with the other on its right gives way. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Semi-circular rule**: determined by magnetic track (not true). Odd levels eastbound/0-179, even levels westbound/180-359 for IFR; VFR adds 500 ft. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **VFR restrictions**: height over obstacles, cloud and visibility minima, seaward distance, FL150 ceiling, daylight window. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Refuelling safety**: building/person 15 m, smoking/naked flame 30 m, jet blast 43 m. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_fair)
- **Night rules**: navigation light arcs (red 110 port, green 110 starboard, white 140 rear) and the night definition. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Aerobatics**: height and distance limits. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Formation**: pre-arranged, ATC conditions in controlled airspace; passenger restriction. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_fair)

## Common Mistakes

- **Night definition vs logging**: The Vinod definition of night is "half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise" (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). The VFR daylight window is different: "20 minutes before sunrise to 20 minutes after sunset" (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Do not swap these two windows.
- **Refuelling distances**: 15 m (building/person) vs 30 m (smoking/naked flame) vs 43 m (jet blast) (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). The fair notes give 15 m and 30 m only (src: air_regulations_fair).
- **Semi-circular vs RVSM above FL290**: RVSM same-track separation is 2000 ft, but the non-RVSM same-track value cited is 4000 ft (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Do not apply the 1000 ft RVSM figure to non-RVSM traffic.
- **Converging traffic**: the aircraft with the other on its right gives way; the aircraft on the right has right of way (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). Do not reverse to "left".
- **Head-on**: both aircraft alter heading to the RIGHT (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Formation flying**: zenithaviation_regsnotes says "No formation Flights for Civil Aircraft", while air_regulations_fair says formation is allowed with ATC permission but never with passengers. Treat the two as conflicting in the source set.
- **Minimum IFR level**: 1000 ft (elsewhere) vs 2000 ft (high terrain/mountains) above the highest obstacle within 8 km (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Airspace hazard**: The Rashtrapati Bhavan prohibited-area height is "50000'" in zenithaviation_regsnotes but "unlimited upper level" in notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav. Do not quote one as definitive without checking the AIP.
- **VMC Class B vertical distance**: air_regulations_4 uses 1000 ft in its general VMC answers but 300 m in its Class B answers. The values are close but are written differently; quote the exact unit the question uses.
- **Lowest FL vs semi-circular start**: the pool and sources use "FL290" as the RVSM/semi-circular boundary; do not mix it with FL150 (the VFR ceiling) or FL100 (used as a VMC threshold in zenithaviation_regsnotes).

## Revision Summary

- Right of way: emergency first; converging - the aircraft with the other on its right gives way; head-on - both turn right; overtaken aircraft keeps course. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Semi-circular: magnetic track 0-179 = odd FL (IFR) / odd FL + 500 ft (VFR); 180-359 = even. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- RVSM: FL290 to FL410, 1000 ft separation. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- India level limits: lowest FL050, highest available FL450, maximum FL460. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- VFR: not above FL150; not 100 NM seaward in controlled airspace; daylight 20 min before sunrise to 20 min after sunset. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- VFR minimum heights: 300 m (1000 ft) over congested areas (600 m radius), 150 m (500 ft) elsewhere. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Refuelling: 15 m building/person, 30 m smoking/naked flame, 43 m jet blast. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Aerobatics: 2000 ft AGL completion height; 2 NM from aerodrome perimeter; 600 m from built-up areas. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- Night lights: red port 110 degrees, green starboard 110 degrees, white rear 140 degrees. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- Minimum IFR level: 1000 ft (elsewhere) / 2000 ft (high terrain) above obstacle within 8 km. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
