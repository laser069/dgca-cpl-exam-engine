## Definition

- Licences and ratings are granted, renewed or varied by the Central Government, which may withhold them if it considers it desirable (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, National Provisions "Licensing authority").
- Personnel licensing is governed by ICAO Annex 1 (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). DGCA is the licensing authority that issues licences (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- A cross-country flight (general) is a flight to a point beyond a 100 NM radius of the departure aerodrome, over land or sea. For PPL it is beyond 50 NM (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Carriage of operating crew: a private aircraft must be flown by a holder of a valid pilot's licence; a public transport aircraft by a commercial, senior commercial or ATPL holder (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Formation

Background / how it works:

- Each licence has four blocks: education (age/schooling), knowledge (exams), experience (flight hours), medical. Then validity and renewal rules apply (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Licences are valid from the date of issue. Renewal runs from the date of expiry if the application is made within one month of expiry (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Medical validity starts from the day of the medical exam (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- A holder who has an illness or injury causing incapacity may not exercise licence privileges until declared fit after a fresh medical (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 42).
- Pilots' flight-time limits are cumulative (7 / 30 / 365 days). Duty-time limits (FDTL) depend on crew complement and day/night operation (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).

## DGCA Important Facts

**Age and education**
- Minimum ages: SPL 16; PPL 17; CPL 18; ATPL 21 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- ATPL: minimum 21, maximum 65 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). CPL: maximum 65 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Maximum age for a pilot acting as PIC or co-pilot in commercial air transport: 65 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 28A).
- Education: SPL and PPL need Class 10 pass; CPL and ATPL need 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Under 16 may not have sole control of an aircraft in motion (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 28).

**Validity and renewal**
- PPL is valid 10 years; all other licences 5 years (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- SPL validity: 5 years or until a higher licence is obtained; renewal after 5 years (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- Student licence renewal: up to 12 months from fresh medical, total licence validity not exceeding 24 months from issue (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Instrument rating validity: 1 year (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes; air_regulations_4 Q90, matched).

**Medical**
- Medical assessment: PPL 24 months; ATPL and CPL 12 months. Where 12 months applies it reduces to 6 months after age 40 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Zenith gives the same 6 month rule above 40 for CPL and ATPL and "1 year below 40" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Illness reporting: holder must inform the Authority of an incapacity that lasts 15 days or more (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Zenith gives "consecutive illness of 15 days or hospitalisation of 7 days" (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes). air_regulations_4 Q94 keys 21 days (matched; JAR-based).

**Experience (aeroplane)**
- PPL: 40 hours total; at least 20 solo; 5 cross-country; 10 solo within 12 months (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Quick notes: 40 total, 20 PIC (150 NM X-CTY), 5 night, 5 instrument (2.5 on approved sim), 10 PIC in last 12 months (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- PPL privilege limit: no flight for remuneration; night passengers only with night rating (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CPL: 200 hours within 5 years, of which 100 PIC; 20 cross-country PIC including a 300 NM flight with full-stop landings at two aerodromes; 5 night PIC (10 take-offs and 10 landings); 10 instrument hours (max 5 on simulator) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- CPL with IR: 200 total; 100 PIC; 50 cross-country PIC incl 300 NM; 40 instrument (max 20 on simulator) (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- CPL privilege: act as PIC of aircraft endorsed in licence with AUW up to 5700 kg (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- ATPL: 1500 total; 500 PIC (of which 200 cross-country and 50 night); 1000 cross-country; 100 night; 100 instrument (max 50 simulated) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Within the 1500 hours, 150 hours in preceding 12 months and 10 hours in preceding 6 months (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- ATPL knowledge: Air Regulations, Air Navigation, Avionics, Meteorology. A holder of a current CPL need not pass Air Regulations (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Night rating (PPL): 50 PIC hours incl 5 hours night with 5 take-offs and 5 landings (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

**Pass marks**
- SPL oral exam pass mark 50% (src: air_regulations_quick_notes). PPL, CPL, DGCA written: 70% (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).

**Privileges and restrictions**
- SPL: fly under supervision; VFR only; no passengers, cargo or animals; no cross-country unless 10 hours solo and oral exams in Air Navigation and Met passed (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; air_regulations_quick_notes).
- PPL: no flight for remuneration; night passengers only with valid night rating; cannot fly IFR without valid rating (src: air_regulations_quick_notes; notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- PIC, co-pilot, instructor: flight instructor rating needed for dual instruction; no other person may impart instruction without written DG authorisation (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Flight navigator carried by public transport aircraft flying over 600 NM great circle without landing, if not equipped with navigation equipment giving instant and continuous position (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Cabin crew carriage by seat count: 10-49 seats 1; 50-99 2; 100-149 3; 150-199 4; 200-249 5; 250-299 6 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).

**Flight time and duty limits (DGCA sources)**
- Maximum 125 hours in any 30 consecutive days; co-pilot time counts 80% (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 42A). Zenith: co-pilot counted 50% for licence renewal, 80% otherwise (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Maximum 1000 hours in 365 consecutive days; 125 hours in 30 days; 35 hours in 7 days (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Daily maximum (Vinod, two-pilot): 8 hours (one crew) or 9 hours (two-pilot, day/night); landings capped at 6 (day) and 3 or 2 (night) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Zenith table: domestic 2-crew day flight time 8, duty 11, landings 6; night flight time 9, duty 12, landings 3 (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Records of fatigue system kept 18 months (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Consecutive night duty: no crew may fly passenger flights if they flew between 0000 and 0500 the previous day (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Log books: all flight time logged in ink; certified quarterly (end of March, June, September, December); preserved for not less than 5 years after the last entry (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav; zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Alcohol and psychoactive drugs: crew may not take them within 12 hours of commencement of flight (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, rule 24; air_regulations_fair).

## Common Mistakes

- **Illness reporting: 15 vs 21 days.** notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav and zenithaviation_regsnotes say 15 days (zenith adds 7 days hospitalisation). air_regulations_4 Q94/Q95/Q141 key 21 days (JAR-FCL). Use the DGCA source for DGCA questions; flag the JAR key.
- **Medical validity: 24 months vs 12 months.** PPL is 24 months; CPL and ATPL are 12 months. Both halve at age 40 (notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Zenith gives "1 yr below 40" for CPL.
- **Co-pilot credit: 50% vs 80%.** 50% = renewal credit (zenithaviation_regsnotes; air_regulations_4 Q96). 80% = 125-hour rule (notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav rule 42A) and "otherwise" credit in zenith.
- **Licence validity: PPL 10 years vs others 5.** Do not apply 10 years to CPL/ATPL (notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Minimum ages:** SPL 16, PPL 17, CPL 18, ATPL 21. Pool questions mix PPL and CPL minima (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Pass marks:** SPL 50%, PPL/CPL DGCA 70% (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- **PPL vs CPL privilege:** PPL = no remuneration; CPL = commercial flying, PIC up to 5700 kg (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
- **Day vs night landings:** domestic day 6 landings, night 3 (zenith) vs Vinod's 3 and 2 for two-pilot night operations. Use the table that matches the question's crew/day-night wording.
- **Cross-country definitions:** 100 NM (general) vs 50 NM (PPL) vs 300 NM (CPL leg) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Student pilot cross-country:** needs 10 hours solo and exams in Air Navigation and Meteorology (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **ATPL "150 hours in 12 months" vs "10 hours in 6 months":** both are separate requirements, not alternatives (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Pool Q "Montreal/JAR" vs DGCA:** air_regulations_4 is JAR-FCL oriented; zenith and Vinod are DGCA-oriented. Do not transfer JAR figures (e.g., 60/24/12 month JAR medicals, 1 year type ratings) to DGCA questions without a DGCA source.
