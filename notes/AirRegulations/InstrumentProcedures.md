## Definition

- **Instrument approach procedure**: "A series of predetermined manoeuvres by reference to flight instruments with specified protection from obstacles from the initial approach fix or where applicable, from the beginning of a defined arrival to a point from which a landing can be completed..." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Precision approach**: "A direct instrument approach using bearing, elevation and distance information" (src: air_regulations_4).
- **Decision altitude / height (DA/DH)**: "The specified altitude, or height in the precision approach at which a missed approach must be initiated if the required visual references to continue the approach has not been established." DA is referenced to mean sea level and DH to threshold elevation. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Minimum descent altitude / height (MDA/MDH)**: "A specified altitude or height in a non-precision approach or circling approach below which descent must not be made." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Missed approach point (MAPt)**: "The point in an instrument approach at or before which the prescribed missed approach procedures must be initiated in order to ensure that minimum obstacle clearance is not infringed." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Obstacle clearance altitude/height (OCA/OCH)**: "The lowest altitude or alternatively the lowest height above ... for establishing compliance with appropriate obstacle clearance criteria." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Minimum sector altitude (MSA) / terminal arrival altitude (TAA)**: "provide at least 300 m (1000 ft) obstacle clearance within 46 km (25 NM) of the navigation aid." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Holding pattern**: entry is heading-based; standard holding turns are to the right; "Descending or climbing in a holding pattern is called shuttle." (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## Classification

- **Approach types**: precision approach (PA); non-precision approach with FAF (NPA with FAF); NPA without FAF; approach with vertical guidance (APV); circling (visual manoeuvring) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Approach segments (air_regulations_4 answer)**: arrival, initial, intermediate, final, missed approach (src: air_regulations_4).
- **Precision approach categories**: CAT I, CAT II, CAT IIIA, CAT IIIB, CAT IIIC (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Missed approach phases**: initial, intermediate, final (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Holding entry sectors**: sector 1 (parallel), sector 2 (offset), sector 3 (direct) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Minimum IFR altitude basis**: high terrain/mountains vs elsewhere (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Formation

How an instrument procedure is built up (background from the sources):

- A procedure has an arrival segment (STAR), an initial approach segment, an intermediate segment, a final segment and a missed approach segment. The arrival route allows transition from en route to approach (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4).
- The final approach segment starts at the FAF (non-precision) or the final approach point (FAP) where the intermediate altitude intercepts the nominal glide path (precision) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Obstacle clearance is built in: the missed approach has a minimum climb gradient and phase-by-phase clearance (initial 300 m, intermediate 30 m, final 50 m in the answer key set) (src: air_regulations_4, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Holding protects aircraft in a defined pattern; standard entry is by heading relative to three entry sectors with a zone of flexibility on the sector boundaries (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Circling protection is a visual manoeuvre area, with the OCA/OCH set for the circling area (src: air_regulations_4).

## DGCA Important Facts

- Approach segments: arrival, initial, intermediate, final, missed approach (src: air_regulations_4).
- Missed approach phases: initial phase begins at the MAPt and ends at the start of climb (SOC) with no turns; intermediate phase begins at SOC; final phase begins where 50 m (164 ft) obstacle clearance is first obtained (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Missed approach must be initiated not lower than DA/H for precision and not lower than MDA/H for non-precision (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Standard missed approach climb gradient: 2.5 per cent (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Obstacle clearance margins (answer-key set in air_regulations_4): initial approach primary area at least 300 m (984 ft); missed approach intermediate phase 30 m (98 ft); missed approach final phase 50 m (164 ft) (src: air_regulations_4).
- Final approach fix (FAF): optimum distance from threshold 9.3 km (5.0 NM); maximum normally 19 km (10 NM) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- FAF descent gradient example: 5.2 per cent / 3.0 degrees (52 m/km, 318 ft/NM) (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Precision approach: intermediate altitude intercepts the glide path between 300 m (1000 ft) and 900 m (3000 ft) above runway elevation; for a 3 degree glide path, interception occurs 6 km (3 NM) to 19 km (10 NM) from threshold (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CAT I: decision height not lower than 60 m (200 ft); visibility not less than 800 m or RVR not less than 550 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CAT II: DH lower than 60 m (200 ft) but not lower than 30 m (100 ft); RVR not less than 300 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CAT IIIA: DH lower than 30 m (100 ft) or no DH; RVR not less than 175 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CAT IIIB: DH lower than 15 m (50 ft) or no DH; RVR less than 175 m but not less than 50 m (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- CAT IIIC: no DH and no RVR limitation (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Non-precision or circling approach: MDH cannot be lower than the OCH (src: air_regulations_4).
- Descent below MDA/H during circling: only when visual reference is established and can be maintained, and the required obstacle clearance and landing position can be achieved (src: air_regulations_4).
- Circling: maximum airspeed for Cat B aeroplane within the protection envelope: 135 kt (src: air_regulations_4).
- Holding turns: standard holding patterns use right-hand turns; all turns at 25 degree bank or 3 degrees per second, whichever requires the lesser bank (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4).
- Holding outbound time (still air): 1 minute at or below 14,000 ft; 1 minute 30 seconds above 14,000 ft (src: air_regulations_4).
- Holding entry: heading-based relative to three entry sectors; zone of flexibility of 5 degrees either side of sector boundaries (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Holding speed for a jet entering a holding pattern at FL110: 230 kt IAS (src: air_regulations_4).
- Holding buffer area beyond the holding area boundary: 5 NM (src: air_regulations_4).
- Holding separation: 5 minutes flying time from the holding area, or the prescribed distance of 5 NM (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- Holding: if unable to comply with the standard pattern, advise ATC as early as possible (src: air_regulations_4).
- Minimum sector altitude: 300 m (1000 ft) obstacle clearance within 46 km (25 NM) of the navigation aid (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav); air_regulations_4 gives 300 m within 25 NM of the homing facility (src: air_regulations_4).
- Radar identification (no SSR): heading changes of 30 degrees or more (src: air_regulations_4).
- Speed adjustment on radar-controlled intermediate and final approach: not more than +/- 20 kt (src: air_regulations_4).
- Transition altitude: normally specified for each aerodrome by the State; lowest transition altitude in India is 4000 ft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- Transition altitude: not less than 3000 ft AGL (src: air_regulations_4).
- Transition level: lowest flight level available for use above the transition altitude (src: air_regulations_4).
- Transition layer: altitude reported as altitude (QNH) below transition altitude, and as flight level above transition level (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_4).
- Above transition level the vertical position is expressed as flight level (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes); the flight level basis is standard setting 1013.25 hPa (src: air_regulations_4).
- Procedure turn: 45 degree turn away from outbound track, then 180 degree turn (src: air_regulations_4).

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- **Segment names**: arrival, initial, intermediate, final, missed approach. "Arrival" is not a missed approach phase; the missed approach phases are initial, intermediate, final (src: air_regulations_4, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Obstacle clearance chain**: primary area of initial approach 300 m; missed approach 30 m (intermediate) and 50 m (final). (src: air_regulations_4)
- **DA vs MDA**: DA/DH for precision; MDA/MDH for non-precision and circling; missed approach starts no lower than these values. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **CAT categories**: DH and RVR limits as listed above. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Holding timing**: 1 minute outbound up to 14,000 ft; 1.5 minutes above 14,000 ft in still air; turns right in standard pattern. (src: air_regulations_4, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Holding entry**: three sectors by heading; 5 degree flexibility zone. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Descent through transition layer**: reporting altitude below transition altitude; flight level above transition level. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_4)
- **Radar approach control**: speed adjustments and heading changes are limited (+/- 20 kt; heading change 30 degrees). (src: air_regulations_4)

## Common Mistakes

- **Missed approach phases**: "arrival" is not a missed approach phase; the phases are initial, intermediate and final (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4). Pool option B that adds "Arrival" is a trap.
- **DA vs DH reference**: DA is referenced to mean sea level; DH is referenced to threshold elevation (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **MDA vs MDH**: the source defines "Minimum Descend Altitude (Height)" as one concept with two expressions (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes); DA is referenced to MSL and DH to threshold elevation (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). For non-precision or circling approach, MDH cannot be lower than OCH (src: air_regulations_4).
- **Holding outbound**: 1 minute at or below 14,000 ft; 1 minute 30 seconds above 14,000 ft (src: air_regulations_4). Do not use 1 minute above FL140 or 1.5 minutes below.
- **Holding turn direction**: standard holding is right-hand; non-standard holding turns are left-hand (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Holding entry**: entry is heading-based (three sectors), with a 5 degree flexibility zone (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Do not describe entry by timing.
- **Missed approach climb gradient**: 2.5 % is the normal gradient (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Pool options 3.3 % or 5 % are not the source figure.
- **CAT I DH**: the lowest DH for CAT I is 60 m (200 ft); CAT II starts below 200 ft and goes to 100 ft; CAT IIIA is below 100 ft (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).
- **Transition altitude (India) vs minimum**: 4000 ft is the lowest transition altitude in India (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav); "not less than 3000 ft AGL" is the general minimum (src: air_regulations_4). They are different concepts.
- **Circling speed**: Cat B max 135 kt for circling in the protection envelope (src: air_regulations_4). Do not confuse with the 230 kt holding speed at FL110 (src: air_regulations_4).
- **MSA radius**: 25 NM (46 km) for MSA (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4).
- **Intermediate approach**: begins at the inbound track of the procedure turn, base turn or final inbound leg of a racetrack; ends at FAF or FAP (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav).

## Revision Summary

- Five segments: arrival, initial, intermediate, final, missed approach. (src: air_regulations_4)
- Missed approach phases: initial (MAPt to SOC, no turns), intermediate (from SOC), final (50 m / 164 ft clearance). (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)

- MOC: initial primary 300 m (984 ft); missed approach intermediate 30 m (98 ft); final 50 m (164 ft). (src: air_regulations_4)
