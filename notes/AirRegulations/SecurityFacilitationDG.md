# SecurityFacilitationDG

## Definition

- **Security (ICAO Annex 17):** "a combination of measures and human and material resources intended to safeguard international civil aviation against acts of unlawful interference." (src: air_regulations_4)
- **Annex 17 title:** "Security: Safeguarding International Civil Aviation Against Acts of Unlawful Interference." (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Unlawful interference:** an act such as hijacking or unlawful seizure of an aircraft. An aircraft being subjected to it is treated as in a state of emergency. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh)
- **Facilitation (ICAO Annex 9):** procedures that speed the entry, clearance and departure of aircraft, persons, baggage and cargo in international air transport. Covered in the notes through the general declaration, cargo manifest and passenger/crew documents. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Security restricted area:** airside area where security measures apply. Airside and non-airside passengers must not mix. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Screening:** application of means to identify prohibited articles. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)

## Classification

- **Security (Annex 17):** safeguarding against unlawful interference; covers aircraft, passengers, baggage, cargo and airport areas. (src: air_regulations_4, air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Facilitation (Annex 9):** documents, clearance, customs and immigration procedures. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Dangerous goods (Annex 18):** safe transport of dangerous goods by air. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Hague-Tokyo-Montreal-Warsaw-Rome conventions:** air-law conventions relevant to security and liability (src: air_regulations_quick_notes, notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes).
  - Tokyo: offences on board against penal law (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
  - Hague (Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Seizure of Aircraft): states agree to prohibit and punish hijacking (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
  - Montreal: offences on board leading to damage of aircraft property, unlawful violent act (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
  - Warsaw: responsibility for carriage of passengers, baggage and cargo (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).
  - Rome: damage caused by foreign aircraft to third parties on the surface (src: air_regulations_quick_notes).

## Formation

- **ICAO requirement:** each contracting state must establish a national civil aviation security programme. The state designates an appropriate authority to develop, implement and maintain it. (src: air_regulations_4)
- **Operator security:** the operator should have a written security programme and an approved security training programme. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Airport security:** each airport should have a written security programme, assisted by an airport security committee. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Indian security rules:** Aircraft Security Rules 2011 (airport security) and Aircraft (Carriage of Dangerous Goods) Rules 2003 are referred to in the notes. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **In-flight procedure on unlawful interference:** set SSR code 7500; the aircraft should endeavour to notify the appropriate ATS unit of the fact and any deviation from the flight plan. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Level change when unlawful interference and no regional procedure applies:** change level by 150 m (500 ft) where 300 m (1000 ft) vertical separation applies, or by 300 m (1000 ft) where 600 m (2000 ft) applies. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav)
- **Hijack level guidance (Indian notes):** squawk 7500 and fly 500 ft above or below the assigned altitude when below FL290, and 1000 ft above FL290. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh)
- **Isolated parking:** an aircraft subjected to unlawful interference is parked in an isolated bay at least 100 m from other parking bays. (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Facilitation documents:** general declaration, cargo manifest, and a list of goods in cabin. Each is presented in 2 or 3 copies at departure, handwritten in block letters in ink. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)

## DGCA Important Facts

- **Carriage of arms and explosives (dangerous goods):** only with written permission from the Central Government. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Carriage of mails:** only with permission of the Director General of Post and Telegraph (DGPT). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Carriage of prisoners:** with permission of DGCA, DDG (Deputy Director General), DRI (Director of Regulation and Information) and CAD (Civil Aviation Department). (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Armed personnel on board:** allowed only if all States involved agree; PIC must be notified of their number and location. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Hijack squawk:** 7500 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes, air_regulations_4).
- **Emergency squawk:** 7700. Radio communication failure: 7600. (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Aircraft transit without landing:** passengers in a contracting state who lack a connecting flight may remain for up to 2 days. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Notice for landing at international airports:** flights landing without prior information must give at least 2 hours' advance notice. (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes)
- **Transit without visa:** no visa required for transit under 2 days (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536). Exit visas are not required in the same source.
- **Unidentified baggage:** baggage not picked up or identified by a passenger is reported to the operator and PIC. Usually boarded before passengers. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)
- **Re-screening:** if airside and non-airside passengers mix, the passengers and their cabin baggage must be re-screened before boarding. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536, air_regulations_4)
- **Security threat:** the PIC, police or airport manager is contacted if a security threat is present. (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536)

## Frequently Tested Concepts

- **Transponder for hijack:** Mode A code 7500. Emergency is 7700; communication failure is 7600 (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, air_regulations_4).
- **Hijack in-flight:** notify ATS if possible; level change as above; FL290 is the boundary in the Indian notes (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
- **Annex mapping:** Annex 9 = Facilitation; Annex 17 = Security; Annex 18 = Dangerous goods (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav). Zenith uses different numbers (see Common Mistakes).
- **Convention mapping:** Tokyo = offences on board; Hague = unlawful seizure; Montreal = offences on board causing damage to aircraft property; Warsaw = carriage liability; Rome = third-party damage on surface.
- **Who authorises what:** arms and explosives (Central Government); mails (DGPT); prisoners (DGCA, DDG, DRI, CAD).
- **Isolated parking and hijacked aircraft:** 100 m from other parking positions.
- **Air-side vs non-air-side:** passengers must not mix; re-screen if they do.
- **PIC responsibilities:** the PIC has authority over safety and security of the aircraft (src: dgca_regs_cpl_syllabus).

## Common Mistakes

- **Hijack vs emergency vs radio failure:** 7500 is hijack/unlawful interference; 7700 is emergency; 7600 is radio failure. Do not swap them (src: notes_air_regs_vinod_yadav, zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Annex numbering conflict:** zenithaviation_regsnotes lists "Annex 13 Facilitation" and "Annex 17 Accident/Incident Investigation" and "Annex 9 Units of measurement". Vinod Yadav and Nirmal Firosh and the DGCA syllabus list Annex 9 = Facilitation, Annex 13 = Accident investigation, Annex 17 = Security. CONFLICT. Use the DGCA syllabus numbering (src: dgca_regs_cpl_syllabus).
- **Convention names:** Tokyo covers on-board offences against penal law; Hague covers unlawful seizure; Montreal covers on-board offences causing damage to aircraft property; Warsaw covers carriage liability; Rome covers third-party damage. Do not interchange Hague and Montreal.
- **Arms and explosives:** the Central Government gives written permission. DGCA alone does not authorise carriage of arms and explosives (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Isolated parking:** 100 m from other bays, not 1000 m or 3000 m (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh, zenithaviation_regsnotes).
- **Descent level for hijack:** below FL290 the notes say 500 ft above or below; above FL290 it is 1000 ft. Do not apply the 1000 ft rule below FL290 (src: notes_air_regs_nirmal_firosh).
- **Re-screening:** mixing airside and non-airside passengers requires re-screening, not only a documentary check (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536).
- **Transit days:** 2 days for passengers without a connecting flight (src: zenithaviation_regsnotes); visa exemption for transit under 2 days (src: air_law_keys_notes_230527_222536). Do not merge the two.

## Revision Summary

- Security = measures and resources to safeguard civil aviation from unlawful interference (Annex 17).
- Facilitation = Annex 9 procedures for documents, clearance and passenger flow.
- Hijack squawk 7500; emergency 7700; comms failure 7600.
- Hijack in flight: notify ATS if possible; fly 500 ft off assigned level below FL290 and 1000 ft above FL290 (Indian notes).
- Isolated parking bay at least 100 m from other bays.
- Arms and explosives: Central Government written permission; mails: DGPT; prisoners: DGCA/DDG/DRI/CAD.
- Transit passengers without a connecting flight may stay up to 2 days.
- Non-scheduled international landings give at least 2 hours' notice.
- Airside and non-airside passengers must not mix; re-screen if they do.
- Treat the zenith annex labels as a conflict; the DGCA syllabus numbering is the reference.
